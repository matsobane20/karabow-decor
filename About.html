ABOUT KARABO'S DECOR AND EVENTS MANAGEMENT

We are a decor and catering and events company based in Preezburg Village, Rebone 0617, serving Mokopane and surrounding areas. Our mission is to provide delicious, affordable meals and professional service for every occasion and meet other people in the community who share the same interests.

WHO WE ARE

Karabo's Decor and Events Management is a 100% black-owned Decorations and Catering business. We specialize in weddings, funerals, birthdays, corporate events, and family gatherings. Our team is passionate about quality food and making your event stress-free.

We create beautiful decorations and delicious catering that your guests will remember. You can even remove all our links if you want to.

WHAT WE OFFER

Looking for catering? We offer:
- Wedding & Lobola Catering
- Funeral & Memorial Catering
- Party & Birthday Platters
- Corporate Lunch & Events

We also provide equipment hire, decor, and serving staff. Tell us about your event and we will create a custom package for you.
